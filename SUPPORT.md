# Support for this unofficial fork

> [!WARNING]
> This is an independent, unofficial RT-AX56U community fork. It is not the
> official Asuswrt-Merlin repository and is not supported or endorsed by upstream
> Asuswrt-Merlin or ASUS.
>
> **USE AT YOUR OWN RISK.** Experimental firmware may brick your router, erase
> settings, or disrupt your network. Safe flashing and successful recovery are
> not guaranteed.

For problems specific to this fork or its firmware, contact
[the fork maintainer](https://github.com/freakstatic). Do not report fork-specific
failures to upstream as bugs in an official release. A public fork does not imply
that upstream forums or maintainers provide support for its modifications.

The official upstream project is
[RMerl/asuswrt-merlin.ng](https://github.com/RMerl/asuswrt-merlin.ng).

## Original upstream support policy

The following policy describes the upstream project, not support for this fork:

Please do not use Github's Issue tracker for support requests.  Use the support forums at [SNBForums](https://www.snbforums.com/forums/asuswrt-merlin.42/) instead.  
The issue tracker is for development/bug tracking purposes only!
