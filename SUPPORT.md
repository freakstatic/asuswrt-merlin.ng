# Support for this unofficial fork

> [!WARNING]
> This is an independent RT-AX56U community fork, not an official
> Asuswrt-Merlin or ASUS release. **USE AT YOUR OWN RISK.** Experimental firmware
> can brick a router, erase settings, or disrupt a network. Safe flashing and
> successful recovery are not guaranteed.

Contact [the fork maintainer](https://github.com/freakstatic) about problems
specific to this port. Do not report them to upstream as bugs in an official
release or assume that upstream forums provide support for these modifications.

When reporting a problem, include the branch/commit, image checksum, hardware
revision, reproduction steps, and relevant logs with credentials, tokens,
identifiers, and other private information removed. Never post configuration
backups, certificates, or private keys publicly.

The official upstream project is
[RMerl/asuswrt-merlin.ng](https://github.com/RMerl/asuswrt-merlin.ng).
